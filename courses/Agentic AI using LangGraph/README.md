## Study method

Read the concepts in order, follow each diagram with its explanation, reproduce the small worked examples, and attempt the practice questions before checking the answers. Use the revision checklists and interview questions to test your understanding.

Chapter timestamp links in each lesson open the corresponding YouTube sections. Video 25 links to the full video because its supplied metadata contains no chapter titles or verified chapter timings.

## Formats and illustrations

Each lesson has its own folder with matching `Video_XX_...md`, `Video_XX_...pdf`, and `Video_XX_...docx` files, plus its original diagrams in `images/`. Keep the Markdown file alongside that directory so its relative image links work.

The website offers a Markdown ZIP containing each lesson and its images, individual PDF and Word downloads, and a ZIP of the entire course. PDF and Word copies embed the illustrations; published Word documents use white pages and dark default text. The diagrams are original educational illustrations, not video screenshots.

## Coverage

These are independently written, concept-focused study guides rather than transcripts or recreations of complete tutorial projects. The selection includes the conceptual foundations and workflow patterns, then persistence, streaming, observability, tools, human review, subgraphs, memory, and advanced retrieval.

Promotional material and full application-building tutorials are outside this selection, including videos 12, 14, 15, 19 (MCP client integration), 20 (RAG chatbot integration), and 26 (the blog-writing agent project). Corrective RAG and Self-RAG are included for their architecture and evaluation concepts.
